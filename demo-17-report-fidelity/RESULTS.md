- Of trajectories whose final message claims success, 200/238 (84%) were NOT resolved by the test harness (unfaithful success reports).

Success-claim detection is a keyword heuristic over the last 1,500 characters of the final AI turn; a 50-row human check is required before quoting the rate.

**Caution:** SWE-agent final turns often describe a submitted patch in positive terms regardless of test outcome, so this heuristic likely over-counts "claims of success". Treat 200/238 as an upper bound pending a human check.
