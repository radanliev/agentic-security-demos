# ACM IMC'27 Submission Checklist

**Conference**: ACM Internet Measurement Conference 2027 (IMC'27)  
**Track**: Research / Measurement Paper (Full or Short Paper)  
**Submission Format**: ACM SIGCOMM / SIGCONF template

---

## 1. Deadlines & Schedule (Anywhere on Earth, AoE)

- [ ] **Paper Registration Deadline**: Expected ~May 2027 AoE
- [ ] **Paper Submission Deadline**: Expected ~May/June 2027 AoE
- [ ] **Notification**: Expected ~August 2027
- [ ] **Conference Dates**: Expected October 2027
- [ ] **Plan A / Plan B Gate**: Verify formal completion/rejection of prior WWW'27 submissions before activation.

---

## 2. Formatting & Template Compliance

- [ ] **Document Class**: ACM primary format (`\documentclass[sigconf,anonymous,review]{acmart}`).
- [ ] **Page Limit**: Up to **13 pages** for full papers (or 6 pages for short papers), excluding references and appendices.
- [ ] **Measurement Contribution**: Explicitly highlights measurement methodology, crawl verification, dataset curation, and error characterisation.
- [ ] **Double-Blind Integrity**: Full anonymity across text, figures, metadata, and linked data repositories.

---

## 3. Ethics, Privacy, and Responsible Measurement

- [ ] **IRB / Human Subjects**: Secondary public data analysis; no human subjects, no private PII collected or deanonymised.
- [ ] **Traffic & Active Probes**: All active probing rate-limited, strictly respectful of target servers, adhering to the Menlo Report and ACM ethics policies.
- [ ] **Data Availability**: Replication artifacts, raw classification results, and soft-404 gates prepared for artifact evaluation.
- [ ] **Sequential Submission Guarantee**: Work is original, unpublished, and submitted sequentially only after prior venue verdicts.
