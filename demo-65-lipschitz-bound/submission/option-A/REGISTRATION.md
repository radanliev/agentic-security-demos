**Title:** Spectral-Norm Lipschitz Bounds for Two-Layer Linear Maps

**Abstract (DRAFT):** The Lipschitz constant of a layered linear map is bounded by the product of the spectral norms of its factor matrices, and that product is itself bounded above by the product of the corresponding Frobenius norms. This article records the closed-form spectral norm of a two-by-two real matrix and uses it to compute the product bound exactly on small factors, making the gap between the spectral product and the Frobenius product visible. The calculation is a neural-network analysis tool, not an application paper about a security product. Public image benchmarks whose licences permit research use, including Fashion-MNIST under the MIT licence, are identified as later training substrates for comparing constrained and unconstrained multilayer perceptrons. A companion teaching demonstration evaluates eight labelled synthetic matrix pairs. No empirical training result is claimed here.

**Keywords:** Lipschitz constant, spectral norm, neural networks, certified robustness, linear maps
