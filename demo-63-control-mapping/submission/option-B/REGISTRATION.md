**Title:** Knowledge-Base Alignment of Adversary Techniques to Compensating Controls

**Abstract (DRAFT):** Public cybersecurity knowledge bases describe adversary techniques and, separately, catalogues of compensating controls, but the two are maintained as distinct artefacts. This article specifies a knowledge-engineering procedure that aligns each in-scope technique to a set of compensating controls and then, for a declared system profile, returns the union of those controls together with the techniques that remain uncovered. The procedure is a deterministic set computation, not a learned ranker. It is designed for the publicly licensed MITRE ATT&CK knowledge base, used under MITRE's published terms, together with NIST Special Publication 800-53, which is a United States government work. A companion teaching demonstration applies the same alignment rules to eight labelled synthetic profiles. No coverage claim about a live enterprise is made.

**Keywords:** knowledge engineering, intelligent systems, control mapping, MITRE ATT&CK, NIST SP 800-53
