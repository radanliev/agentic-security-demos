# ACL 2027 (ARR Jan 2027) Submission Checklist

**Venue**: 65th Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics (ACL 2027)  
**Submission Mechanism**: ACL Rolling Review (ARR) — January 2027 Cycle  
**Submission Portal**: OpenReview (`https://openreview.net/group?id=aclweb.org/ACL/ARR/2027/January`)

---

## 1. Deadlines & Schedule (Anywhere on Earth, AoE)

- [ ] **ARR January 2027 Submission Deadline**: ~15 January 2027 23:59 AoE
- [ ] **Reviews & Meta-Reviews Returned**: Mid-February 2027
- [ ] **Commitment to ACL 2027**: Early April 2027
- [ ] **Acceptance Notification**: Late April 2027

---

## 2. Formatting & Template Compliance

- [ ] **Document Class**: Standard ACL template (`\documentclass[11pt]{article}` with `acl` style).
- [ ] **Page Limit**:
  - Long paper: Up to **8 pages** of main content (excluding Limitations, Ethics Statement, references, and appendices).
  - Short paper: Up to **4 pages** of main content.
- [ ] **Mandatory Sections**:
  - `Limitations` section explicitly included after main text.
  - `Ethics Statement` explicitly included after main text.
- [ ] **References**: Unlimited pages for references following the main body.

---

## 3. Anonymity & Double-Blind Policy

- [ ] **Strict Anonymisation**: No author names, institutional affiliations, or acknowledgments in PDF.
- [ ] **Third-Person Citations**: Self-citations referenced in the third person.
- [ ] **Code / Artifact Anonymisation**: Clean links without identifying repository names or author identities.
- [ ] **ARR Anonymity Window**: Conforms to ACL policy on preprints and social media discussion during review.

---

## 4. Sequential Submission Gate & Preconditions

- [ ] **WWW'27 Decision Released**: Formal rejection decision received from The Web Conference 2027 (expected 04 January 2027).
- [ ] **Zero Concurrency**: Ensure no active review status at WWW'27 before registering on ARR OpenReview.
- [ ] **Reviewer Feedback Incorporation**: Reviewer comments from WWW'27 integrated to strengthen NLP/CL contributions.
