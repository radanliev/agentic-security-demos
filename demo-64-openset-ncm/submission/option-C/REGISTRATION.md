**Title:** Open-Set Nearest-Class-Mean Rejection for Histogram Patterns

**Abstract (DRAFT):** Forced nearest-neighbour assignment always names a known class, even when a pattern lies far from every class mean. This article studies a classical nearest-class-mean rule with a distance-to-mean rejection threshold, so that a query can be labelled unknown when it exceeds the threshold, and compares that rule with a forced one-nearest neighbour that cannot reject. The contribution is a pattern-recognition decision rule and its behaviour on histogram-style feature vectors, not a new neural architecture. Public pattern corpora whose licences permit research use, including Fashion-MNIST under the MIT licence, are identified as evaluation substrates for a later confirmatory study. A companion teaching demonstration scores eight labelled synthetic histograms. No claim is made about accuracy on any external corpus.

**Keywords:** open-set recognition, nearest class mean, histogram features, rejection threshold, pattern classification
